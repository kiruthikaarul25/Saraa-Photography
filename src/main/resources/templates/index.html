Our story

The photograph you forget was being taken

Saara Photography began with a simple idea: the best photograph is the one you forget was being taken.

We sit with each family before the day, learn who laughs loudest and who cries first, and then stay out of the way.

Every album is edited by hand, so the colours match what you remember.
